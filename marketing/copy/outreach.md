# Outreach emails

Short, personal, no attachments on the first email, no money offered. Send from your own address.
Find the right contact on each person's own site or channel "About" page. Send no more than 5 a
week so each one is written properly; personalise the first line every time.

---

## 1. Scam-awareness creators (YouTube, Twitch, TikTok)

**Subject:** A free check for viewers who already let a scammer in

> Hi [name],
>
> I watched your [specific video] where [specific moment]. The question I keep seeing in
> comments under videos like that is "I already let one in last month. How do I know they're
> gone?"
>
> I built a free, read-only tool for that question: PacketCheck. It's a small script for
> Windows, Mac and Linux that lists remote-control apps (AnyDesk, ScreenConnect, UltraViewer…),
> Remote Desktop settings, startup items and live connections, then explains them in the
> browser. It changes nothing and uploads nothing, and the code is public on GitHub.
>
> If it's ever useful to point viewers at, it's at packetcheck.net. I'm not asking for a
> sponsorship slot. I'd be grateful for any feedback on what it misses; you've seen more of
> these setups than anyone.
>
> [you]
